# 10 · Vision · DuodooTekr

**DuodooTekr** (多度科技) is a Nanjing-based technology company. We combine **open-source ERP/MES practice** with **field-proven remote monitoring** for water and pump infrastructure—especially where cellular coverage is weak or unreliable.

## What we believe

- **Open components where it helps trust** — manuals, samples, and verifiable building blocks for due diligence; project delivery stays tailored to your site and compliance rules.
- **Phased go-live beats big-bang ERP** — scope boundaries and acceptance criteria matter as much as software choice.
- **Remote operations are a product problem** — satellite or hybrid backhaul, authorized start/stop, and operations dashboards for utilities and EPCs—not only “another SCADA screen.”

## What we do

| Pillar | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| **Remote IoT** | Long-distance water supply, pump stations, weak-network sites, satellite backhaul, monitoring and control patterns for utilities and EPCs |
| **Digital operations** | Odoo-centric ERP rollouts: inventory, finance, HR attendance, manufacturing phases, cross-border operations |
| **Delivery** | Remote and on-site implementation, integration, and advisory—China domestic and overseas projects |

## Product line (overview)

**DuodooTEKr** open stack direction: modular ERP + MES + IoT platform thinking—flexible deployment (cloud or on-prem), customization-friendly architecture. Details vary by contract and industry; public docs show what is already packaged for sharing.
